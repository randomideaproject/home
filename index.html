    How Moth Watch works

    Move in

    Pick a username, then your tower and level. Already joined on another device? Enter the same username and choose "That's me".

    Inside or outside

    Inside means in your apartment. Outside means anywhere around the towers. Tap +1 Inside or +1 Outside to log one moth, or use "Log several at once".

    How scoring works

    • Residents score every moth they log, inside and outside.
    • Levels and towers only score inside moths.
    • If you move, open Settings and change your home. Your moths come with you.

    The moth inspector

    Log a lot of moths in one day and the inspector will have questions.

    Add to your home screen

    iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    Android: open the browser menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

    About the Bogong Moth

    The Bogong moth (Agrotis infusa) is a dark brown Australian moth with a wingspan of 40 to 50 mm. Each spring, from early September, it migrates up to about 965 km from the lowlands to caves in the Australian Alps, where it rests over summer. It returns to its breeding grounds in autumn.

    The moths are food for animals such as the endangered mountain pygmy-possum, and Aboriginal peoples have long gathered them in the mountains. Numbers have fallen sharply since around 1980, and in December 2021 the IUCN listed the species as Endangered. The decline is mainly attributed to drought and rising temperatures in the caves.
